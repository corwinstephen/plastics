There’s great irony in the fact that many of the over-budget and endlessly delayed projects local governments work on are spending money to rob community members of the opportunity to come together and build something for themselves.

People are craving purpose. People are craving physical things. Park the diesel behemoths and let us show up with shovels.

Better for the environment, better for the community, better for the individual both physically and mentally. It’s a single solution to a million problems.
