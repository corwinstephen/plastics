Gulf island sustainability:

In order to save the planet, it’s very important that no recyclables ends up in the trash. Please thoroughly wash all recyclables, then separate them into paper, metal, plastic, and glass. Then, because we’re all taking responsibility for ourselves, we will each INDIVIDUALLY drive 30 kilometers round trip to the recycling center to drop off those recyclables.
