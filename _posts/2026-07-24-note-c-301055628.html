Everyone knows AI is sycophantic at this point, but the ways it manifests keep surprising me. AI will never say no to you, even if what you ask it to do is incoherent. Even if there’s no sense in it at all.

The other day I spoke at length with someone who claimed to be building something profound—some kind of knowledge network that they struggled to articulate when pressed and eventually told me “I just needed to see it.”

The prompt history was borderline schizophrenic nonsense. It read like a conspiracy theorist rattling off a series of disparate patterns and asking the system to fill in the gaps between them. There was no real world context and no practical application. But unable to say no, it started building, and the result was an enormous network of vector concepts that, despite their utter meaninglessness, “fit” together in a coherent, runnable system. It was essentially a massive “hello world” application, but with so much code that the author believed they had created something extraordinary.
